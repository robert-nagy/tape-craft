On issue #5 an unattended run chose JavaScript, Fastify and a flat layout in the RFC without asking. The maintainer wanted C# with React/TypeScript and a named architecture, so the PR had to be rebuilt. Follow docs/adr/0001-stack-and-architecture.md (vertical slices around a pure domain). When a change needs a stack, architecture or folder-structure decision the ADRs do not cover, stop and ask the maintainer, even in sentinel-sdlc-auto, and record the answer as a new ADR.
